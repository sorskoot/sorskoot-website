# Website Privacy

This page covers the website timmykokke.com. The privacy policy for my games is on a [separate page]({{< ref "privacy-policy.md" >}}).

This website does not track you. There are no analytics, no advertising, no cookies, no social media widgets and no comment system. I don't collect, store or sell any personal data through the site.

## What happens when you visit

- Everything on the site (pages, images, fonts and scripts) is served from timmykokke.com itself. Your browser does not contact Google, Disqus, ShareThis or any other third party just because you read a page.
- The hosting provider keeps standard server logs (such as IP address, time, requested page and browser) to keep the site running and secure. These logs are kept for 3 days. I don't use them to track visitors.
- The site registers a service worker that caches pages and assets in your own browser, so the site loads faster and works offline. This data stays on your device. You can remove it at any time by clearing your site data.

## Embedded content and links

- Videos (YouTube), Twitch clips and live demos (StackBlitz) are not loaded automatically. You see a placeholder first, and the third-party player only loads after you click it. From that moment, that provider receives your IP address and may set cookies, under its own privacy policy. You can also use the link on the placeholder to open the content on the provider's site instead.
- Share links (LinkedIn, Bluesky, X, email) and the "Support me" link to Ko-fi are plain links. They only contact those services if you click them.
- Other links on this site lead to external websites that have their own privacy policies.
