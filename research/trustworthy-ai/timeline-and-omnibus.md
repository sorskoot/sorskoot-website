## Confirmed dates

- 2 Feb 2025: "prohibited AI practices and AI literacy obligations entered into application from 2 February 2025". Also: "Prohibitions 1-8 became effective in February 2025."
- 2 Aug 2025: "the governance rules and the obligations for GPAI models became applicable on 2 August 2025".
- 27 July 2026: "On 27 July 2026, the AI Omnibus enters into force across the EU". Proposed 19 Nov 2025, political agreement 7 May 2026.
- 2 Aug 2026: "The AI Act entered into force on 1 August 2024 and became applicable on 2 August 2026, with some exceptions." Also: "The transparency rules of the AI Act will come into effect in August 2026." No Article 50 or watermarking delay is mentioned on either page.
- 2 Dec 2027: Annex III high-risk: "Starting on 2 December 2027, high-risk AI systems will be subject to strict obligations".
- 2 Aug 2028: Annex I high-risk: "extended transition period until 2 August 2028".

## Additions and differences

- **Prohibition 9 (new, Omnibus):** "Prohibition 9 comes into effect in December 2026". Covers non-consensual sexually explicit content and CSAM. Article 5 page says "From 2 December 2026, two additional prohibitions apply" (points (ba) and (bb)).
- **AI literacy simplified by the Omnibus:** "Previous AI literacy requirement for companies is simplified, with the Commission and the Member States taking a stronger role in promoting AI literacy."
- **Enforcement date:** policy page governance section: "From 2 August 2026, the AI Office and authorities of the Member States are responsible for implementing, supervising and enforcing the AI Act." The prohibited practices guidelines summary says penalties and national authorities apply since 2 Aug 2025. These two sources differ and are not reconciled here.
- **Other Omnibus changes (no dates on the page):** some SME measures extended to small mid-caps; more sandboxes incl. an EU-level one; simplified registration of exempted systems; processing special category data to detect and correct bias allowed; extended AI Office oversight of some systems built on GPAI models in large platforms; clearer interplay with other EU law.

## Not verified

## Follow-up (fetched 2026-11-04): official implementation timeline, Art. 99, Art. 113

Sources:
- https://ai-act-service-desk.ec.europa.eu/en/ai-act/timeline/timeline-implementation-eu-ai-act (takes the Omnibus into account)
- https://ai-act-service-desk.ec.europa.eu/en/ai-act/article-99
- https://ai-act-service-desk.ec.europa.eu/en/ai-act/article-113

- 2 Feb 2025: "General provisions (definitions & AI literacy) and prohibitions apply."
- 2 Aug 2025: "Rules for general-purpose AI apply and governance must be in place." "Member States need to designate national competent authorities and adopt national laws on penalties."
- 2 Aug 2026: "The majority of rules of the AI Act come into force and enforcement starts for applicable rules." "Transparency rules (Article 50) start to apply." "Enforcement of the AI Act starts at national and EU-level concerning general-purpose AI models, prohibitions, transparency rules and AI literacy."
- 2 Dec 2026: "New prohibitions + Article 50(2) transition apply." New prohibitions: non-consensual sexual deepfakes and CSAM. Transition: "Transitional deadline for certain providers of AI systems, including GPAI systems, generating synthetic content, that were already placed on the market before 02 Aug 2026, to comply with Article 50(2)." The page does not say whether it covers systems placed on the market after 2 Aug 2026.
- 2 Aug 2027: at least one AI regulatory sandbox per Member State.
- 2 Dec 2027 / 2 Aug 2028: Annex III / Annex I high-risk rules.
- Art. 113: general rule "It shall apply from 2 August 2026." Chapters I and II from 2 Feb 2025, except Art. 5(1) points (ba) and (bb) and 5(1a), (1b), from 2 Dec 2026. Art. 101 (GPAI fines) has no date on the page.
- Art. 99(3): "up to EUR 35 000 000 or, if the offender is an undertaking, up to 7 % of its total worldwide annual turnover for the preceding financial year, whichever is higher." SMEs incl. start-ups: whichever is lower (99(6)).

Resolves the earlier difference: penalty laws had to be in place by 2 Aug 2025 (matches the guidelines summary), actual enforcement starts 2 Aug 2026.
